## 🇺🇸 English

### App name (max 30)
Shape Survivor

### Short description (max 80)
Survive endless swarms of shapes! Shoot, level up, and evolve in this arcade roguelite.

### Full description (max 4000)
◆ SHAPE SURVIVOR ◆
Start as a single tiny triangle and survive against endless swarms of shapes.

Just move with one finger — your weapons fire automatically. Defeat enemies, collect gems to level up, and pick a different upgrade every time to craft your own build. Bullets and explosions fill the screen, and a powerful boss shows up in every stage — one short, intense run after another.

■ How to play
· One-hand control — drag to move, attacks are automatic
· Choose weapon upgrades, new weapons, or stats on every level-up
· Weapon evolution — meet the conditions to awaken a stronger form
· Class system — Ranger, Warrior, Mage, Guardian
· Stage bosses — charger, summoner, bullet-hell types
· Roguelite growth — permanent upgrades make each run stronger, even after you die
· Spend gold to permanently upgrade your starting weapon and abilities

■ Perfect for you if
· You want intense fun in short sessions
· You love the vampire-survivor / survivor genre
· You enjoy the feel of growing and evolving

Survive with a single shape — starting now!

* Playable offline, no internet connection required.
